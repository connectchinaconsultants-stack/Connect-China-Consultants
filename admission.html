Admission Process

A practical overview of intakes, documents, timelines, university charges, and consultancy fees — so students and parents can plan with clarity.

Admission intakes

Chinese universities typically have two admission intakes: March and September. Some universities only accept students for the September intake. Availability depends on the specific university and program.

Contact us timely, to get the latest information about admission intakes and requirements.

Step 1: Submit Document Set 1 and Admission Fee

Students submit the initial documents and pay the admission fee. The expected processing time for the admission letter and JW letter is approximately 10–15 days.

Document Set 1

  • Passport.
  • Photographs with a white or blue background.
  • SSC and HSSC certificates or O/A Level report cards, with a minimum of 60% marks or equivalent.
  • Police clearance certificate.
  • Bank statement.
  • Foreigner Medical Form, which will be provided.

Step 2: Submit Document Set 2 and Apply for the Study Visa

After receiving the admission letter and JW letter, students submit the remaining documents and proceed with their Chinese study visa application. The expected visa preparation and processing period is a little over one month. Actual timing depends on the relevant authorities and individual circumstances.

Document Set 2

  • SSC and HSSC certificates or O/A Level certificates Attested by IBBC and Ministry of Foreign Affairs.
  • Police clearance certificate.
  • Bank statement Attested by IBBC and Ministry of Foreign Affairs.
  • FRC form.
  • Admission letter and JW letter.

University admission documents and visa application requirements are separate stages of the process.

Step 3: Prepare for Departure

Connect China Consultants helps students prepare for their journey, including luggage, flight and train tickets booking, travel essentials, accommodation-related preparation, and practical advice for living and studying in China.

Step 4: Arrival and University Transfer

The consultancy provides support before and after the flight. Representatives pick up and drop off students at their respective universities and assist them until they settle comfortably.

Admission fees and transparency

Payments to universities are separate from consultancy service fees. Do not treat the figures below as a substitute for a written offer from the university.

University admission fee

RMB 2,000

Per university. Non-refundable. Paid toward university admission processing.

The supplied brief states that admission is guaranteed if all documents are complete. This is an owner-supplied claim requiring review of the actual policy and its terms before it is presented as an unconditional guarantee.

Service and management fee

RMB 5,000

Charged when the student’s visa has been issued. Covers pickup, drop-off, and support until the student is comfortably settled.

University tuition

Paid to the university

Paid by the student directly to the respective university. Connect China Consultants provides help if students experience payment-related difficulties.

Additional consultancy fees

The consultancy states that it will not charge any additional fees beyond those described above. Payment methods and refund policies are not listed here because they were not supplied in the brief.

Reconfirm fees, document lists, and processing times with Connect China Consultants and the relevant university before submitting an application.
