Degrees

Structured information on MBBS, nursing, and Chinese language study. Licensing, recognition, and tuition vary by university and destination country.

Program A

MBBS / Clinical Medicine

Degree title: Bachelor of Medicine, Bachelor of Surgery.

A 6-year undergraduate medical degree comprising 5 years of classroom and clinical coursework plus 1 year of hospital internship. English-medium instruction is available for international students at partner universities, with strong clinical training at affiliated hospitals.

Claims that graduates receive a WHO-recognized Chinese medical degree and are eligible for USMLE, PLAB, AMC, and similar exams — and that all authorized medical schools in China are recognized by international medical councils and ECFMG — require verification. Not every institution or program automatically qualifies graduates for international licensing.

RecognitionWHO-listed — verify for the particular institution and program.
ApprovalPMC-approved — confirm the intended regulator and correct terminology (PMC/PMDC).
InstructionEnglish or Chinese.
Duration6 years: 5 years of study and 1 year of internship.
IntakesMarch and September, subject to university.
TuitionApproximately RMB 20,000–35,000 per year, varying by university.
EntryFSc/12th grade, pre-medical.
English testsIELTS/TOEFL often not required, subject to university requirements.

Program B

B.Sc. Nursing

Degree title: Bachelor of Science in Nursing.

A 4-year undergraduate program covering human anatomy, physiology, pharmacology, microbiology, community health, and clinical nursing practice. Programs prepare students for nursing careers through structured academic coursework and practical clinical training. Many Chinese universities offer international nursing programs taught in English or Chinese, sometimes with English support. Hospital internships are typically included in later years.

Professional registration and licensing depend on the applicable destination-country requirements. An “RN Eligible” label is not used here because program-specific confirmation has not been supplied.

Duration4 years.
HighlightsFour-year degree, clinical training, and nursing career preparation.
InstructionEnglish or Chinese.
IntakesMarch and September, subject to university.
TuitionApproximately RMB 18,000–28,000 per year; placeholder requiring confirmation.
EntryFSc/12th grade, science.
InternshipIncluded in the final year, subject to program structure.

Program C

Chinese Language and Culture

A program focusing on Mandarin Chinese language, literature, and culture. It is designed to help international students develop Chinese proficiency through language instruction, HSK preparation, and cultural immersion. It may suit students interested in translation, education, international business, or further academic studies in China. Preliminary Chinese-language learning may also form part of other international degree programs.

Language proficiency and examination outcomes are not guaranteed; they depend on starting level, attendance, and program design.

HighlightsHSK preparation, cultural immersion, and career pathways.
Duration6 months to 1 year.
LanguageChinese with English support.
IntakesMarch and September, subject to university.
EntryHigh-school diploma.
Expected outcomeHSK Level 4–5 proficiency, depending on starting level and program — not guaranteed.
